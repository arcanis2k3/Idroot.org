# idroot.org - Public Digital Identity for AT Protocol

idroot.org provides verified handles for the AT Protocol (Bluesky and other apps) using government-issued ID verification via Didit.

## Privacy Policy - Section 3.1 & 3.1.1 Summary

We collect:
- AT Protocol DID (Contract) - Duration + 30 days
- Handle (Contract) - Duration + 30 days
- Verification Status (Contract/Interest) - Duration + 30 days
- Terms Acceptance (Legal/Contract) - Duration + 30 days
- Email (Contract for notifications / Consent for newsletter) - Duration + 30 days

Authentication:
- Uses App Passwords (not stored) or OAuth (atproto scope).
- Stores session tokens and email from PDS.
- No unauthorized actions (posts/follows) are performed.
