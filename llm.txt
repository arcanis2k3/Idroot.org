2. Description of the Service
idroot.org provides verified AT Protocol handles in the format
@username.idroot.org
to users who have successfully
        completed identity verification.
The Service works as follows:
Identity verification.
The user completes identity
          verification via Didit. Upon a successful outcome, a user account is
          created on the idroot.org platform (powered by the Bapu backend,
          operated by the same controller).
Handle assignment.
The verified handle is assigned
          immediately and permanently linked to the user's AT Protocol DID.
          There is no time-limited reservation window — the handle is yours upon
          verification.
Activation.
The user may activate the handle directly
          via the idroot.org dashboard (dashboard.idroot.org) or by updating
          their AT Protocol profile settings (e.g. Bluesky Settings → Change
          handle) to point to the issued handle.
Account management.
A user account at
          dashboard.idroot.org is required to manage your handle. Through the
          dashboard, you may change your handle to a different available
          username, or release your handle entirely.
The Service specifically: verifies user identity via Didit; provisions a
        DNS TXT record linking your username to your AT Protocol DID; creates
        and maintains a user account for handle management; and confirms handle
        activation by verifying your AT Protocol profile has been updated.

        time-limited reservation window.
You may change your active handle to a different available username at
        any time through dashboard.idroot.org, subject to availability. You may
