2. Description of the Service

idroot.org provides free AT Protocol handles in the format @username.idroot.org to users who have successfully completed identity verification. The Service verifies user identity via Didit, provisions a DNS TXT record linking your username to your AT Protocol DID, reserves handles for 48 hours pending activation, and confirms activation by verifying your AT Protocol profile has been updated to use the issued handle.

3. Eligibility

You must meet the minimum age required by your jurisdiction to enter into a binding agreement and to submit to identity verification. In all cases you must be at least 16 years of age. You must be able to complete identity verification with a valid government-issued photo ID and must not be located in a jurisdiction where use of this Service is prohibited by law.

4. Handle reservation and activation

Upon successful identity verification, your chosen handle is reserved for 48 hours. The DNS TXT record is provisioned immediately. You must update your AT Protocol profile (e.g. Bluesky Settings → Change handle) to use the new handle within this window. Activation is confirmed automatically when the Service resolves your handle and verifies it maps to your DID. If not confirmed within 48 hours, the reservation expires and the handle is released.
