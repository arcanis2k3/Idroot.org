2. Description of the Service

idroot.org provides free, verified AT Protocol handles in the format @username.idroot.org to users who have successfully completed identity verification.

The Service works as follows:

  1. Identity verification. The user completes identity verification via Didit. Upon a successful outcome, a user account is created on the idroot.org platform (powered by the Bapu backend, operated by the same controller).
  2. Handle assignment. The verified handle is assigned immediately and permanently linked to the user's AT Protocol DID. There is no time-limited reservation window — the handle is yours upon verification.
  3. Activation. The user may activate the handle directly via the idroot.org dashboard (dashboard.idroot.org) or by updating their AT Protocol profile settings (e.g. Bluesky Settings → Change handle) to point to the issued handle.
  4. Account management. A user account at dashboard.idroot.org is required to manage your handle. Through the dashboard, you may change your handle to a different available username, or release your handle entirely.

The Service specifically: verifies user identity via Didit; provisions a DNS TXT record linking your username to your AT Protocol DID; creates and maintains a user account for handle management; and confirms handle activation by verifying your AT Protocol profile has been updated.

Upon successful identity verification, your chosen handle is assigned immediately. The DNS TXT record is provisioned without delay. You may change your active handle to a different available username at any time through dashboard.idroot.org, subject to availability. You may also release your handle at any time; released handles become available to other verified users.
